Toll Plaza & Weigh Bridge Analytics Dashboard
Organization: Shajar Roads Limited  
Reporting period: 14 August 2023 -- 28 August 2026  
Platform: Microsoft Power BI Desktop
Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard for analysing
toll plaza and weighbridge operations at Shajar Roads Limited. It brings
together revenue, vehicle traffic, vehicle-category trends, payment
status, unpaid traffic, and check-post performance in a consolidated
reporting view.
The dashboard is designed to support operational monitoring,
year-on-year comparisons, revenue tracking, and data-informed
decision-making.

Toll Plaza Analysis
The Toll Plaza section includes:
Revenue comparison by month and fiscal year.
Year-on-year revenue and vehicle-volume growth.
Daily vehicle-volume trends, with notable dates and events
annotated.
Vehicle-category trends, including cars/jeeps, rigid trucks,
trailers, tractor trolleys, buses, pickups/vans, and other
categories.
Average daily vehicle volume by toll plaza, hour, and shift.
Vehicle-volume comparisons across fiscal years.
Unpaid vehicle trends, including lawyer and non-lawyer
classifications.
Full-route, half-route, one-sided, and unidentified traffic
distribution.
Monthly and historical unpaid toll percentages, with entry-side and
exit-side comparisons.
Weighbridge Analysis
The Weighbridge section includes:
Revenue and vehicle-volume year-on-year growth.
Revenue by weighbridge: WB-1, WB-2, WB-3, and WB-4.
Monthly revenue and vehicle-volume comparisons by fiscal year.
Fined and non-fined vehicle-volume trends.
Charged-vehicle ratios.
Vehicle-category volume trends, including:
2 Axle Single (2AS)
3 Axle Single (3AS)
3 Axle Tandem (3AT)
4 Axle Single-Tandem (4AST)
5 Axle Single-Tridem (5AS-Tr)
5 Axle Single-Tandem-Tridem (5AST-Tr)
6 Axle Tandem-Tridem (6AT-Tr)
Trolley and other displayed categories
Paid/unpaid status comparisons by vehicle category and fiscal year.
Current-year versus previous-year vehicle-volume comparisons.
Check-Post Analysis
The report also presents:
Revenue by check-post name.
Average daily revenue by check post.
Check-post comparisons for the locations and labels shown in the
dashboard.

Reporting Period and Interpretation
The PDF report covers 14 August 2023 to 28 August 2026. Some
individual visuals show their own date ranges or fiscal-year
comparisons. The latest fiscal year in the report is partial, ending on
28 August 2026; therefore, comparisons involving that year should be
interpreted in light of the shorter reporting period.
Values and percentages in this README are taken from the supplied
dashboard PDF. They have not been independently recalculated or audited.
